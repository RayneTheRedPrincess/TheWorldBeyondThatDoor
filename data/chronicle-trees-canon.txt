THAT WORLD BEYOND THE DOOR (TWBTD)
RECONSTRUCTION C — NORMAL CLASS CHRONICLE TREES
Approved: 2026-08-07
Status: APPROVED / CANONICAL

APPROVED GLOBAL RULES
- Normal class Chronicle trees are base-class-only.
- No normal class Chronicle node may reference, enhance, copy, unlock, or require a subclass ability, subclass passive, subclass resource, or subclass-specific Kept Impression, EXCEPT the single Warrior Mastery node described below.
- Chronicle nodes strengthen the base class passive/resource and the five approved base-class abilities.
- In all node text below, phrases such as 'Warrior ability', 'Rogue damaging ability', 'Druid heal', and similar class references mean the five base-class abilities only; they never include subclass abilities.
- If a base resource continues to exist while a subclass is equipped, a Chronicle modification to that base resource may still naturally exist; however, no Chronicle effect may target a subclass-specific mechanic or ability.
- Early Chronicle nodes are intentionally simple and obvious. Later nodes become increasingly mechanical, conditional, and identity-defining.
- Each normal tree remains exactly 32 nodes: 10 Core nodes + three 7-node base-class disciplines + 1 Mastery.
- Core nodes cost 1 Chronicle Point each.
- Within each 7-node discipline, node costs are 1 / 1 / 1 / 2 / 2 / 2 / 3 Chronicle Points in order.
- Mastery costs 3 Chronicle Points.
- Full-tree cost is 49 Chronicle Points; maximum spend remains 30 Chronicle Points.
- Warrior Mastery is the sole exception to the normal base-class-only rule: it preserves the approved one-subclass-ability selection/return behavior while remaining a Chronicle feature outside the Character Level ability track.
- Classless is not redesigned here. Its dedicated Chronicle remains separate because Classless is not one of the 11 normal base-class trees.

TREE PREREQUISITES
- Core nodes are the early foundation layer.
- A discipline may be entered after at least 3 Core nodes are purchased and Chronicle Rank 8 is reached.
- Discipline nodes are linear within their discipline.
- Discipline nodes 6-7 additionally require Chronicle Rank 18.
- Mastery requires Chronicle Rank 24, at least 18 Chronicle Points spent in that class tree, and at least 5 purchased Core nodes.

WARRIOR
Scaling: STR / CON
Base resource/mechanic: Pressure

CORE — 10 × 1 Chronicle Point
1. Heavy Hands — Driving Blow deals +8% final damage.
2. Set the Brace — Brace grants +18% Block Chance instead of +15%.
3. Harden the Brace — Brace grants +20% Blocked Damage Reduction instead of +15%.
4. Break Their Guard — Break Line reduces Block Chance and Blocked Damage Reduction by 12 percentage points instead of 10.
5. Hold Strong — Hold Fast creates a Shield 10% stronger.
6. Draw the Eye — While Hold Fast's Shield remains, its Aggro bonus is +25% instead of +20%.
7. Ready Pressure — Start combat with 1 Pressure instead of 0.
8. Under Pressure: Force — Under Pressure grants +12% damage dealt instead of +10%.
9. Under Pressure: Guard — Under Pressure grants +12% Block Chance instead of +10%.
10. Breaker Training — Pressure Break deals +8% final damage.

ASSAULT DISCIPLINE — costs 1 / 1 / 1 / 2 / 2 / 2 / 3 Chronicle Points
1. Driving Opening — Driving Blow deals +10% final damage to a target currently affected by Break Line.
2. Follow the Fracture — A Critical Hit with Driving Blow against a target affected by Break Line reduces Break Line's remaining cooldown by 1 turn, once per turn.
3. Crumpling Line — Break Line also causes the target to deal -5% final damage until the start of your next turn.
4. Breaker’s Window — After Break Line hits, your next Pressure Break against that target within 2 turns gains +10% Crit Chance.
5. No Recovery — Pressure Break against a target affected by Break Line extends Break Line's remaining duration by 1 turn.
6. Guard Collapse — Break Line becomes Unblockable against a target already affected by Break Line.
7. Rupture the Line — At 5 Pressure, Pressure Break against a target affected by Break Line gains +15% final damage and ignores that target's Block Chance.

BULWARK DISCIPLINE — costs 1 / 1 / 1 / 2 / 2 / 2 / 3 Chronicle Points
1. Braced Hold — Casting Hold Fast while Brace is active makes Hold Fast's Shield 15% stronger.
2. Guarded Pressure — If Brace contributes to at least one successful Block before your next turn, gain 1 additional Pressure at the start of your next turn. This can trigger once per Brace.
3. Firm Footing — While any Hold Fast Shield remains, gain +5 percentage points Blocked Damage Reduction.
4. Return Force — After you successfully Block direct damage, your next Driving Blow before the end of your next turn deals +15% final damage.
5. Shielded Momentum — The first time each Hold Fast Shield absorbs damage, reduce Brace's remaining cooldown by 1 turn.
6. Immovable — While Brace is active and a Hold Fast Shield remains, enemy effects cannot reduce your Block Chance or Blocked Damage Reduction.
7. Hold the Line — When a Hold Fast Shield is completely broken by direct enemy damage, immediately gain 2 Pressure and your next Brace before the end of your next turn costs 0 Energy. Once per Hold Fast cast.

PRESSURE CONTROL DISCIPLINE — costs 1 / 1 / 1 / 2 / 2 / 2 / 3 Chronicle Points
1. Pressure Temper — When you first reach 5 Pressure, gain +5 percentage points Blocked Damage Reduction until your next turn.
2. Built Momentum — If you both deal direct Warrior damage and successfully Block during the same turn cycle, your next Warrior damaging ability deals +8% final damage.
3. Stored Force — While at 5 Pressure, the first Pressure you would have gained each turn cycle instead gives your next Warrior damaging ability +5% final damage.
4. No Wasted Motion — While at 5 Pressure, the second Pressure you would have gained in the same turn cycle instead reduces the remaining cooldown of one random cooling-down base Warrior ability by 1 turn.
5. Controlled Release — After spending 3 or more Pressure on an ability, gain +10% Block Chance until your next turn.
6. Pressure Reserve — When an ability spends all 5 Pressure, set your Pressure to 1 after that ability resolves.
7. Tempered Momentum — While at 5 Pressure, the first Pressure you would gain each turn cycle also triggers No Wasted Motion in addition to Stored Force. Pressure Reserve sets your Pressure to 2 instead of 1 after an ability spends all 5 Pressure.

MASTERY — 3 Chronicle Points
Warrior Mastery — Choose exactly one ability from Ruinhewer, SteelBearer, or BrandBlade. The chosen ability is available while no Warrior subclass is active and while using a different Warrior subclass. It keeps its normal Energy cost, cooldown, targeting, damage type, scaling, statuses, and other printed mechanics. If the active Warrior subclass is the same subclass that supplied the chosen ability, the Warrior Mastery copy is removed and that subclass's normal copy follows its ordinary Character Level unlock; when that source subclass is no longer active, the Warrior Mastery copy returns. Duplicate copies are never allowed. If the chosen ability itself creates, tracks, or spends a named subclass resource, that resource may exist only as needed to resolve the chosen ability's own printed mechanics; Warrior Mastery does not grant the source subclass passive or unrelated resource-generation rules. The chosen Mastery ability is locked for the duration of an active campaign and may be changed at the Tavern while outside an active campaign.
