THAT WORLD BEYOND THE DOOR (TWBTD)
C — CLASSLESS CHRONICLE
Status: APPROVED / CANONICAL
Approved: 2026-08-07

======================================================================
IDENTITY
======================================================================

Classless comes from the 6-slot Kept Impression KI-182.
Its Chronicle is intentionally NOT a normal class Chronicle tree.

Classless Chronicle grants NO generic combat/stat power.
It has:
- no damage bonuses
- no healing bonuses
- no defense bonuses
- no Energy bonuses
- no Crit bonuses
- no cooldown bonuses
- no branching tree
- no Chronicle Mastery
- no hidden benefits

Its permanent progression exists only to unlock the exact cross-class freedoms below.

======================================================================
PROGRESSION
======================================================================

Classless Chronicle Rank cap: 12
Classless Chronicle Progress required per Rank: 50
Total Progress to complete: 600

Classless has no separate Chronicle Point spending tree.
Unlocks occur automatically at the listed Chronicle Ranks.

Rank 0 — KI-182 baseline
- exactly 2 Base Ability selection slots

Rank 1
- no additional benefit

Rank 2
- unlock the 3rd Base Ability selection slot

Rank 3
- no additional benefit

Rank 4
- unlock Resource Imprint

Rank 5
- no additional benefit

Rank 6
- unlock the 4th Base Ability selection slot

Rank 7
- no additional benefit

Rank 8
- unlock the 1st Subclass Ability selection slot

Rank 9
- no additional benefit

Rank 10
- unlock the 5th Base Ability selection slot

Rank 11
- no additional benefit

Rank 12
- unlock the 2nd Subclass Ability selection slot

FINAL CLASSLESS CAPACITY AT RANK 12:
- exactly 5 Base Ability selections
- exactly 2 Subclass Ability selections
- exactly 1 Resource Imprint
- nothing else from Classless Chronicle

======================================================================
SELECTED ABILITY RULES
======================================================================

Every selected ability retains its original approved:
- Character Level unlock requirement
- Energy cost
- cooldown
- targeting
- damage type
- weapon requirement
- stat scaling
- statuses
- printed mechanics

Chronicle Rank never causes a selected ability to bypass its normal Character Level unlock.
Classless does not grant the normal class/subclass/passive behavior associated with the source of a selected ability except for the minimum resource handling already required by Classless canon to resolve that selected ability.

======================================================================
RESOURCE IMPRINT
======================================================================

At Classless Chronicle Rank 4, Resource Imprint allows exactly ONE chosen actual approved base-class or subclass resource system from an unlocked source.

The Resource Imprint choice:
- is separate from all ability selection slots
- does not grant the source class passive
- does not grant the source subclass passive
- does not grant unrelated source abilities
- does not grant Kept Impressions
- does not grant unrelated source mechanics
- does not grant additional Chronicle bonuses

Resource Imprint grants only the chosen resource system's actual approved tracking, cap/state, generation, spending, decay/reset rules as needed for that resource to function.

If a resource is canonically one linked/composite system, the linked system counts as ONE Resource Imprint. Examples include Quarry + Marks and Form + Cycle.

Do not invent a new resource for a class/subclass that does not actually possess one.

======================================================================
CONFIGURATION
======================================================================

Classless ability selections and Resource Imprint are configured at the Tavern outside an active campaign.
They are locked during an active campaign.
